Shot Finder

Put it on your phone

Thirty seconds, once. After that the Shot Finder sits on your home screen like any other app, ready for the practice green or the next awkward lie.

  1. Open it in SafariGo to find.shortgamelibrary.com. It needs to be Safari, not the Skool app or Instagram.
  2. Tap the Share buttonThe square with an arrow pointing up ⬆, at the bottom of the screen (top right on iPad).
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"Scroll down the list a little if you can't see it.
  4. Tap "Add"The Shot Finder icon appears on your home screen. Done.
  1. Open it in ChromeGo to find.shortgamelibrary.com.
  2. Tap the menuThe three dots ⋮ in the top right corner.
  3. Tap "Install app"On some phones it says "Add to Home screen" instead. Either one works.
  4. Tap "Install"The Shot Finder icon appears on your home screen. Done.

One more thing: the videos open in Skool, so make sure you're logged in to Skool on your phone. Do it once and every video opens straight away.
